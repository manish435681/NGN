NGN Instruction - 01

Train Boarding &
De-boarding Procedure

Applicable in NMRC Aqua Line for NRS-1 Trains

For boarding and de-boarding the train in depot, Train operator shall follow the designated path for to and fro movement from DCC to stabling location & vice-versa and try to avoid crossing of tracks.

I. Train Boarding Procedure

  • 01Before Boarding the train visually inspect, the under frame of the train from both sides to the extent possible.
  • 02TO should board the train through Front/Rear Cab Side Door as per the availability of platform.
  • 03To open Cab side door, operate locking mechanism by rotating the square key anticlockwise.
  • 04Board the cab and close cab side door.

II. Train De-boarding Procedure

  • 01Stable the train at desired location and AUX OFF train as per procedure.
  • 02TO should de-board the train through Cab Side Door.
  • 03After de-boarding, close the Cab Side Door from outside by using hands.

NGN Instruction - 02

AUX ON/OFF Procedure

Applicable in NMRC Aqua Line for NRS-1 Trains

In an Aux Off NRS-1 train, TCMS can be initialized by putting TAS switch to 'ACTIVATE' position, and shut down properly using the TAS sleep command.

1. AUX ON Procedure

Note: - Mascon Key should not be inserting before TCMS is initialize completely.

Step-1 Verify all Cut Out and Selector Switches at NORMAL position.
Step-2 Ensure PCS (Panto control switch) should be at RBP position and Panto mode switch at HW position.
Step-3 Check 110DC supply from Panel#01 in battery indicator gauge.
Step-4 Operate TAS (Train Activation Switch) rotary Switch to ACTIVATE position and hold for 2 sec.
Step-5 Wait around 60 sec for TCMS & DMI to initialization. Login with operator ID.
Step-6 Insert MASCON KEY and rotate anti-clockwise to ON position. (Note: Do not insert before TCMS initializes).
Step-7 Move Mode Selector to STANDBY position.
Step-8 After that pantographs shall raise and check status of Pantograph on Propulsion page of TCMS.
Step-9 Check line voltage and give VCB close command.
Step-10 Check Current Fault screen for any persisting faults.

2. AUX OFF Procedure

NRS-1 Train can be Aux Off only by TAS button available on Operating Panel No. 02 (Operators desk).

Step-1 Move TBC to Bmax position.
Step-2 Open VCB by hardware/software command.
Step-3 Put PCS (panto control switch) to LBP (lower both panto) position from panel no #3 non TO side, panto will get down.
Step-4 Mode selector OFF.
Step-5 Move mascon key clockwise to OFF position and take out MASCON key from Master Controller.
Step-6 Operate TAS rotary switch to Sleep and hold 2 sec from panel no.#02.
Step-7 Wait 60 sec for total shutdown.
Step-8 Tail light will glow steady.

NGN Instruction - 03

Train Preparation Procedure

For Depot / Out station / Mainline Stabled Train (Rev.01)

📋 INSTRUCTION OVERVIEW
This instruction supersedes the previous "Train Preparation" module . It outlines the mandatory procedures for Train Operators (TO) prior to taking charge, board procedures, initial cab checks before induction, and comprehensive service and emergency brake testing protocols.

1. Before Taking Charge of Train

1.1 Initial Sign ON & Equipment Collection

  • 01Upon completion of signing ON procedure in DCC/SCR/CC, TO should confirm Train set, its location .
  • 02Collect the applicable equipment/items (e.g., MASCON key, Square Key, PSL Key, Tetra Set, FAB and Train Log Book) from on duty DC, SC & Crew controller and acknowledge the same .
  • 03The TO shall then proceed to the designated location of the train in accordance with the procedure in vogue prior to the departure of the train .
📖 1.2 Train Log Book Verification
Train Location Verification Action Required
Depot Stabled Trains Ensure Log Book is duly filled and signed by RS Supervisor and verified by Depot Controller .
Out station / Mainline Check the latest fitness certificate in the previous page of the log Book . If valid, write on the top of the first fresh page: "Fitness is valid up to <date> & <time> and train consists is of 4-car/6-car, endorsed on page no. ____" .

2. While Taking Charge of the Train

  • 2.1TO should follow the guidelines for movement in depot/Out Station for his personal safety .
  • 2.2TO should ensure to board the same Train set as authorized by DCC/SCR/CC .
  • 2.3Before boarding the Front Cab, visually inspect the under frame of the train from both the sides in Depot to ensure that no work is in progress .
  • 2.4TO should board the train through Front Cab Side Door .

3. Checks After Boarding (Before Induction)

Once the TO has boarded the train, the Cab light should be switched "ON" by operating the Cab light switch to ON position at the non-driving side panel no#03 and the checks listed below should be carried out initially .

Step-1 Verify all Safety Cut Out and Selector Switches are set to their NORMAL position. Make sure that all MCBs (circuit breakers) are in 'ON' position (except the instructed ones) .
Step-2 Verify Main Reservoir (MR) air pressure status on the MR gauge should be above 8 bars and Battery voltage is above 100 V at Voltmeter/VDC gauge .
Step-3 Insert MASCON key and turn it to the "Unlock"/ON position .
Step-5 Log on to TCMS .
Step-6 Check Driving Status Page on TCMS and check Current Fault Screen for active faults .
Step-7 Check the Bypass list on TCMS. Any of the equipment mentioned in the list should not be bypassed .
Step-8 Verify working of HVACs and Switch ON the same, if not already ON. Ensure that all HVACs are in AUTO mode or as per instruction .
Step-9 Verify Headlights and Marker Lights functioning normally and check wiper status .
Step-10 Perform saloon door operation of both sides and verify door status on TCMS .
Note: In the event of any irregularities found during the check, TO should report to the DCC for depot stable train and to OCC for Outstation/Mainline stabled train .

3.1 & 3.2 Service Brake Test

Testing of brakes shall be done in standstill condition with TBC at Bmax & EBPB testing in front cab .

Step-I Make sure that MR Pressure gauge and Black needle of Brake Cylinder pressure gauge indicates approx. 8-10 bar .
Step-II Move TBC to Coasting position and Mode Selector at Forward/ATP mode, to make sure that the BC pressure decreases to 1.8 bar (Holding Brake pressure) .
Step-III Move Traction Brake controller (TBC) to "Maximum Service Brake" position, check that the Orange needle of the Brake Cylinder (BC) Pressure gauge should show pressure of around 2.4 bars .

3.3 Emergency Brake Test & Departure

🛑 Emergency Brake Test (Standstill)
Step-I Make sure that MR Pressure gauge indicates approx. 8-10 bar .
Step-II Move Traction Brake controller (TBC) to Emergency brake "EM" position, check that Brake Cylinder (BC) Pressure increases to around 3.2 bars. Also a message "Emergency Brake Applied" will pop up on TCMS .
Step-III Move TBC to B-max position, then press the "Emergency Brake Release" switch at the front panel #02 to release the EB, make sure that BC pressure decreased from 3.2 bar to 2.4 bar approximately .
Step-IV Press EBPB-1, 2 then check that Brake Cylinder (BC) Pressure increase to around 3.2 bars. Also a message "Emergency Brake Applied" will pop up on TCMS .
Step-V Release the EB, by resetting the EBPB-1, 2 and pressing the EB release .
🚄 b) TBC Brake Power Feel Test (In Moving Condition)

Apply and release brakes from TBC to check normal brake application in moving condition .

  • iThe test shall be done before reaching main line entry signal for depot stabled train .
  • iiFor out station/ mainline stabled trains, this test can be performed before the approaching PF from siding / approaching next station PF .
⚠️ 4. Reporting & 5. Main Line Departure
  • 4Any deficiencies or failure noticed during pre-departure checks in Depot shall be reported to DCC immediately and noted down in Train Logbook . For Out Station/mainline stabled Train defects or failures noticed shall be immediately reported to OCC and noted down in Train Logbook .
  • 5Carry out the procedure for driving the train to the Main Line as per the extant procedure .

NGN Instruction - 04

Stabling of Train Procedure

For Depot and Out Station Locations

📋 INSTRUCTION OVERVIEW
This procedure details the steps for stabling NRS-1 trains in the depot when not handed over to maintenance staff, stabling procedures for duly manned trains, and the protocols for stabling in outstation locations after completing revenue service.

I. Stabling of Train in Depot

Applicable when train is normally stabled in Depot and not handed over to Maintenance staff.

Step-1 At depot Entry Shunt Signal, TO should seek authorization from DCC as per extant procedure . Confirm with DCC about the location where train is to be stabled .
Step-2 Drive the train, observing & repeating aspect of en-route signals/hand signals and status of points, to the designated location for stabling and stop .
Step-3
  • 1. Move TBC to Bmax position and open VCB .
  • 2. Put PCS (panto control switch) to LBP position from panel no.#03 non TO side .
  • 3. Move mode selector to OFF position then move MASCON key clockwise to OFF position .
  • 4. Operate TAS rotary switch to Sleep position & hold for 2 sec from panel no#2 .
  • 5. Wait for total shutdown .
  • 6. Tail light will glow steady .
Step-4 Leave the cab with TO's equipment (Mascon key, square key, PSL key, train log book & tetra) .
Step-5 Close and lock the Cab side and cab saloon partition door .
Step-6 Leave train by following Train de-boarding Procedure .
Step-7 Sign off in DCC/SCR as per extant procedure .

II. Stabling of Train in Depot/Siding

Applicable when the train is duly manned by TO .

Step-1 Switch OFF all HVACS .
Step-2 TBC on Bmax position with MS to OFF position .
Step-3 Take out MASCON key .

III. Stabling in Outstation Locations

Applicable after completing Revenue Service .

Step-1 Train Operator shall stable train at designated Stabling Point on Main Line as per extant Procedure .
Step-2
When train is stabled unmanned in siding after completing Revenue Service :
  • 1. Open VCB .
  • 2. Put PCS (panto control switch) to LBP position from panel no.#03 non TO side .
  • 3. Move mode selector to OFF position, then move MASCON key clockwise to OFF position .
  • 4. Operate TAS rotary switch to Sleep position & hold 2 sec from panel no.#02 .
  • 5. Wait for total shutdown .
  • 6. Tail light will steady .
Step-3 Leave the cab with TO equipments .
Step-4 Close and lock the Cab side Door .
Step-5 Leave train by following Train de-boarding Procedure .
Step-6 Train Operator shall move between Platform and Siding as per extant procedure .
Step-7 Sign OFF in Crew Control/Station Control Room .

Important Notes for Stabling

⚠️ Mandatory Precautions
  • Do not exit from train until all train interior lighting and TCMS HMI is off (to ensure shutdown of train) .
  • Ensure that no unauthorized person remains on train .
  • In case, train is handed over to Maintenance staff in Depot/Outstation then TO must inform OCC/DCC .

NGN Instruction - 05

Functioning of Safety Cut-Out & Selector Switches

Operating Panel #5 Controls in NRS-1 Trains

This module details the purpose, positions, and operational usage of the various Safety Cut-Out Switches and Selector Switches available on Operating Panel #5 for handling system bypasses and mode configurations during train operations.

I. Safety Cut-Out Switches

Located on Operating Panel #5. Used for bypassing critical safety loops during failures .

01. SCS
Safety Cut-Out Switch (SCS)

Two positions rotary switch with plastic protective cover .

  • NORMAL: Normal operating position .
  • ISOLATE: Used during failure of both VATC systems to bypass ATP/ATO functions . Train will run under TO supervision .
02. RESET ATC
Reset ATC Switch

Two positions rotary switch with plastic protective cover .

  • NORMAL: Normal operating position .
  • ISOLATE: Move to ISOLATE then back to NORMAL to reset the VATC system .
03. CABDBS
Cab Door By-Pass Switch (CABDBS)

Self-Resetting rotary switch with two positions .

  • EDBS: Used when Emergency door lock switch is faulty and parking brake cannot be released .
  • CABBS: Used when Cab side door lock switch is faulty. Bypasses the lock switch .
04. EBBPS
Emergency Brake Bypass Switch (EBBPS)
⚠️ This Switch is not to be used by Train Operator under any circumstance .
  • NORMAL: Normal position .
  • ISOLATE: To bypass Emergency brake loops 1 and 2 .
05. LMRGBS
Low MR Bypass Switch (LMRGBS)
  • NORMAL: Normal position .
  • ISOLATE: Used to bypass main reservoir low pressure to release the emergency brake .
06. PBRBPS
Parking Brake Bypass Switch (PBRBPS)
  • NORMAL: Normal position .
  • ISOLATE: Used in emergency to isolate parking brake fault and move train .
07. SBPS
Service Brake Bypass Switch (SBPS)
  • NORMAL: Normal position .
  • ISOLATE: Used to isolate the service brake fault when ABAR is on and traction is blocked .

II. Selector Switches

Located on Operating Panel #5 .

01. PANMS
Panto Mode Switch (PANMS)
  • HW: Control pantographs via hardwired switches .
  • SW: Control pantographs via TCMS .
02. DMS
Door Mode Switch (DMS)
  • AA (Auto-Auto): Automatic opening/closing in AUTO Mode .
  • AM (Auto-Manual): Automatic opening; closed manually by TO .
  • MM (Manual-Manual): Manual opening and closing by TO .
03. EBLSS
Emergency Brake Loop Selector Switch (EBLSS)

Two position rotary switch with positions: LOOP-1 and LOOP-2 .
04. FLS
Flasher Light Switch (FLS)
  • ON: Turns on the flasher light .
  • OFF: Turns off the flasher light .

NGN Instruction - 06

VCB & PANTO Controls

Operation and Isolation Procedures in NRS-1 Trains

This module details the procedures for manually controlling the Vacuum Circuit Breaker (VCB) and Pantographs using both Software Logic (TCMS soft buttons) and Hardware Logic (Operating Panel switches) .

I. VCB Controls: Software Logic

Using the TCMS interface for VCB operations .

01. TCMS (SW)

Software Logic (Soft Buttons on TCMS)

Accessed via the "VCB CONTROL" menu in the DRIVING STATUS page .

  • OPEN: Press OPEN soft key → Press YES to confirm. This will open both VCBs of the Train .
  • CLOSE: Press CLOSE soft key → Press YES to confirm. This will close both VCBs of the Train .
  • RESET: Press RESET soft key → Press YES to confirm. This will reset tripped VCB/VCBs of the Train .

I. VCB Controls: Hardware Logic

Using the Operating Panel #02 pushbuttons for VCB operations .

02. PANEL (HW)

Hardware Logic (Push Buttons on Panel #02)

VCBs can also be manually OPEN/CLOSE/RESET using Hardware Pushbuttons (VCBOPB) available on Operating Panel #02 .

  • OPEN: Press and release "VCB OPEN" pushbutton. This will open both VCBs of the Train .
  • CLOSE: Press and release "VCB CLOSE" pushbutton. This will close both VCBs of the Train .
  • RESET: Press and release "VCB RESET" pushbutton. This will reset tripped VCB/VCBs of the Train .

I. VCB Isolation & Normalization

⚠️ Operational Rule

A tripped VCB can be isolated only from TCMS in Software Logic mode . Hardware isolation is not possible.

TCMS Control Interface
🔴 Method of VCB Isolation 🟢 Method of Normalizing VCB
  • 1. Open VCB CONTROL Menu on PROPULSION page (located in CAR STATUS page) .
  • 2. Press "ISOLATE" soft button located below the specific DT-Car icon .
  • 3. This will strictly isolate the VCB of that DT-Car only .
  • 1. Navigate to the VCB CONTROL menu on TCMS .
  • 2. To cancel the isolate command, press the "RESET" soft key button .

II. PANTOGRAPH Controls: Software Logic

ℹ️ Operational Note

Controlled via "PANTO" soft button in DRIVING STATUS page. Both pantographs can be controlled simultaneously via ALL UP/ALL DOWN keys .

TCMS Control Interface
⬆️ PANTO UP ⬇️ PANTO DOWN 🔴 ISOLATE
  • Press "UP" soft button located below the target Pantograph .
  • Confirmation window will appear → Press YES to confirm .
  • Press "DOWN" soft button located below the target Pantograph .
  • Confirmation window will appear → Press YES to confirm .
⚠️ Pantograph can be isolated in panto down condition only .
  • Press "DOWN" first, then press "ISOLATE" below the target Pantograph .
  • Confirmation window will appear → Press YES to confirm .

II. PANTOGRAPH Controls: Hardware Logic

⚠️ Logic Selection Note

Hardware Logic uses the Panto Control Switch (PCS) on Panel #3. Software and Hardware Logic for PANTO are NOT active simultaneously (selected via PANMS) .

Panel #3 Control Interface
⚙️ PCS Switch Operations 🔄 Method of Normalizing
4-Position Selection Switch:
  • UP: Put PCS to RBP (Both), RRP (Rear), or RLP (Local) .
  • DOWN: Put PCS to LBP (Lower Both) .
  • ISOLATE: Isolation is NOT possible via hardware. Must use TCMS .
Normalizing Isolated Pantograph:
  • 1. Open PANTO CONTROL menu on DRIVING STATUS page of TCMS .
  • 2. Press "ALL ISOLATE RESET" soft button .
  • 3. Press YES to confirm and reset all isolated pantographs .

NGN Instruction - 07

Single Unit Operation

Operation Procedure for NRS-1 Trains

📋 INSTRUCTION OVERVIEW
This procedure outlines the steps required to isolate a defective pantograph and safely operate an NRS-1 train as a single unit . It details the execution using either Software Logic (TCMS) or Hardware Logic (Operating Panels) .

1. Through Software Logic (TCMS)

Complete procedure to isolate a defective pantograph using the TCMS screen .

Software Control

Software Execution Sequence

Step 1
ACCESS MENU: Open PANTO CONTROL menu on DRIVING STATUS page of TCMS .
Step 2
LOWER PANTO: Press "DOWN" soft button located below the Pantograph that is defective and is to be lowered. Press Yes on confirmation screen .
Step 3
VERIFY DROP (<5kV): Ensure lowering of pantograph by verifying line voltage value <5kv on DRIVING STATUS page .
Step 4
ISOLATE UNIT: Press ISOLATE soft button located below DT car whose pantograph is defective and Press Yes on confirmation screen .
Step 5
VERIFY HEALTHY (~25kV): Ensure Healthy Pantograph is in raised condition by verifying line voltage value around 25kv on DRIVING STATUS page .

2. Through Hardware Logic (Panel #3)

Complete procedure to isolate a defective pantograph using Pushbuttons/Switches on Operating Panels .

Panto Selector Switch

Hardware Execution Sequence

Step 1
ROTATE TO LBP: Rotate Panto Control Switch (PCS) (available on right equipment panel) to LBP (Lower both pantographs) position .
Step 2
VERIFY DROP (<5kV): Ensure lowering of all pantographs by verifying line voltage value <5kv on DRIVING STATUS page .
Step 3
SELECT HEALTHY UNIT: Select healthy unit by rotating PCS (Panto control switch) at desired position .
Step 4
VERIFY HEALTHY (~25kV): Ensure Healthy Pantograph is in raised condition by verifying line voltage value around 25kv on DRIVING STATUS page .
⚠️ Logic Selection Note

Panto control logic (Hardware/Software) can be selected from the Panto mode switch (PANMS) . Software and Hardware Logic for PANTO control are not active simultaneously .

Movement in DBPS
Isolate Position

Action Plan for TO & OCC (Supersedes NGN-11)

📋 INSTRUCTION OVERVIEW
Supersedes NGN-11 . Mandatory protocols for moving a train with the Door Bypass Switch (DBPS) in ISOLATE due to unresolvable door failures (e.g., PECU, faults) leading to traction block/EB . Priority is always resolving the fault at the station .

Core Rules for DBPS Movement

1

Acknowledge TCMS

TO must acknowledge the Door Bypass soft button on the TCMS screen every time the train stops at a station .

2

Verify Before Bypass

TO must visually verify closure of Doors & PSDs before pressing the TCMS bypass button .

3

OCC Authorization

Traffic Controller grants movement permission . Chief Controller escalates to Sr.OCM/OCM & Dy HOD/Ops .

4

Safety Paramount

Utmost caution required . Safety is the highest priority .

Train Operator Responsibilities

Step-1 Troubleshoot & Request: Inform OCC . If fault persists, seek permission to ISOLATE DBPS .
Step-2 Physical Check: Open PF-side cab door .
Step-3 Clear & Command: Verify boarding is clear via mirrors/CCTV . Issue close command .
Step-4 Verify Closure: Confirm all doors/PSDs are physically closed .
Step-5 Seek Assist (If Blind): Use Station Staff to verify closure if visibility is poor .
Step-6 Final OK: Confirm total closure to OCC before departure .
🚄 Mandatory Driving Rules
  • Drive in ATP Mode ONLY (NO ATO) .
  • Apprise relieving TO of DBPS status at handovers .
  • Normalize DBPS switch before leaving cab at terminal stations .

TC & SC Responsibilities

Traffic Controller (TC/OCC)

  • 01 Deploy Staff: Instruct Station Controllers to monitor platforms .
  • 02 Mandate Confirmation: Seek explicit door closure confirmation from TO before departure .
  • 03 Enforce Everywhere: Repeat procedure at every station until restored .

Station Controller (SC)

  • 01 Respond: Reach platform immediately. Notify supervisory booth .
  • 02 Equip: Carry Square key, PSL key, Tetra, and Private No. book .
  • 03 Verify & Exchange: Monitor door closing. Exchange Private Number with TO. Report faults instantly .

NGN Instruction - 12

Wheel Slip/Slide
Detection Guidelines

Action Plans for TO & OCC in NRS-1 Trains

📋 INSTRUCTION OVERVIEW
Wheel slip/slide can occur when rail adhesion becomes poor due to rain, drizzle, early morning dew, or greasy materials on the track . This module outlines the protocols for Normal and Bad weather conditions when the Braking and Propulsion system detects these faults .

Fault Detection & Weather Definition

TCMS Fault Message

When a slip/slide occurs, the following failure message will pop up on the TCMS :

"BECU Fault - WSP active at axle# (# Car)"

Example: "BECU Fault - WSP active at axle1 (DM1 Car)"

🌧️ "Bad Weather" Definition

Weather is officially categorized as "Bad" under the following specific conditions :

  • 01 Early morning hour (before 7:00 AM) of extreme winter .
  • 02 The first hour of occasional winter rains .
  • 03 The first rain mixed with dust during the summer season .

Case-I: Normal Weather Condition

If a wheel slip/slide fault is detected during normal weather, the TO informs OCC and the following guidelines apply .

Step-1 Train Operator shall switch over to ATP mode from ATO in running condition and use manual braking .
Step-2 Apply braking effort as required for controlling the speed of the train or stopping within the stopping window .
Step-3 While entering the station platform, the TO shall drive the train about 5 kmph below the advisory speed .
Step-4 OCC shall instruct the TO to drive the train in non-ATO mode with manual braking up to the next 5 stations .
Step-5 The TO shall give feedback to OCC about the fault at the 5th station . If it did not reappear, OCC instructs TO to resume normal service .
⚠️ Persistent Fault Rule If the slip/slide fault appears again, OCC shall instruct the TO to drive the train up to the terminal (or intermediate) station following guidelines 1 through 4 .

Case-II: Bad Weather Condition

System-Wide Instructions

If a wheel slip/slide fault is reported by a TO during defined bad weather conditions, OCC shall instruct ALL Train Operators to drive their trains following the Case-I manual braking guidelines (ATP Mode, Manual Braking, -5 kmph entry) .

Initial Distance

Trains must be driven under these restrictions up to the next 5 stations or the terminal/intermediate station, whichever is earlier .

Re-Occurrence Protocol

If the fault re-occurs, OCC shall again instruct all TOs to repeat the restrictive driving guidelines for another 5 stations or until the terminal . Otherwise, normal service may be resumed .

NGN Instruction - 15

Troubleshooting Door Open Failures

Actions when Any Door Fails to Open at a Station Platform in NRS-1 Trains

Case 1: Non-opening of few saloon door(s)

Action plan when only specific saloon door(s) fail to open after giving a valid door open command manually or automatically at a station .

Troubleshooting Sequence

Step 1
VERIFY CLOSURE PROVING: After normal alighting and boarding, give a door close command to check if all saloon doors' closing and locking is proved . If yes, depart normally and observe at the next station .
Step 2
RECTIFIED FAULT: If faulty door(s) are rectified at the next station, the train will stay in normal operation for the whole day .
Step 3
PERSISTENT FAULT (ISOLATE): If the fault persists at the next station, TO shall isolate the 'failed to open' door(s) as per extant procedure and paste the relevant sticker on the doors .
Step 4
CLOSURE NOT PROVED (DBPS): If the closing and locking of doors is not getting proved at Step 1 (i.e., DIR-L and DIR-R are not energized), TO shall isolate the doors . If saloon door closing and locking is still not proved, TO shall operate DBPS and operate the train as per extant procedure .
⚠️ DBPS Acknowledgment Rule

In Noida Trains, the TO, in addition to moving the DBPS to ISOLATE position, has to acknowledge the Door Bypass soft button on the TCMS screen every time the train stops at a station .

ℹ️ Verification & Indicator Notes

The TO should verify the closure of Doors and PSD before departure from each station and only then press the Door bypass button on the TCMS screen . The indication lamp (DOL) above the defective door will illuminate permanently due to door isolation .

Case 2: Non-opening of all side doors

Action plan when an entire side of saloon doors fails to open at a station .

Troubleshooting Sequence

Step 1
RETRY COMMANDS: Give door close & open command (maximum three attempts) to check if the doors are opening or not . If saloon doors still do not open, try to open saloon doors in manual/standby mode .
Step 2
SUCCESSFUL MANUAL OPEN: If the doors get opened by manual command, the train will continue in service up to replacement .
Step 3
MANUAL OPEN FAILURE (EVACUATE): In case saloon doors cannot be opened by manual command/door operation in standby by TO, immediate de-boarding of passengers shall take place (through EAD/EED) and the train shall be withdrawn from service .

Emergency Door Operations at Platform

Train EAD, EED & PSD EED Coordination in NRS-1 Trains

This module details the procedure for operating the Train Emergency Access Device (EAD), Emergency Egress Device (EED), and Platform Screen Door (PSD) Emergency Escape Door when a train fails to stop at the correct location and further movement is impossible .

Train & PSD Alignment (Platform Side)

  • When a train is properly aligned with the platform Normal Stopping Point (NSP), all Train EADs will be perfectly aligned with the Emergency Escape Doors (EED) of the PSD system.
  • The PSD EED is a single leaf hinged door opening towards the platform side.
  • From the platform side, it can be opened by inserting a square key into the EED lock and rotating it clockwise for the right EED and anticlockwise for the left EED.
  • Simultaneously, the operator must manually pull the PSD EED towards the platform by grabbing the top side of the EED.

PSD EED Operation (Track Side)

  • The EED may be used in an emergency to de-board passengers if a train could not stop at the correct location and further movement of that train is not possible.
  • From the track side, the Emergency Escape Door can be opened by manually operating a horizontal push bar mechanism.
  • For Automatic Sliding Doors (ASD), emergency release mechanism (ERM) handles are provided on the track side, which can be pushed to manually slide both leaves open.

I. Operating Train EAD from Platform Side

Step-by-step procedure to open the train from the exterior platform side .

Step 1 IDENTIFY DOORS: Identify Saloon Door, with EAD facility, to be opened and also identify its corresponding PSD EED .
Step 2 & 3 UNLOCK EED: Insert square Key in EED lock and rotate key clockwise for right EED and Anticlockwise direction for left EED. Simultaneously, manually pull PSD EED towards platform by grabbing top side of EED. EED will open .
Step 4 ACCESS TRAIN EAD: Open Train EAD box cover .
Step 5 UNLOCK EAD: Rotate EAD clockwise by using square key to unlock position. Take out square key .
Step 6 CLOSE PSD EED: Close EAD box cover and then close PSD EED .
Step 7 OPEN ASD: Insert square Key in ASD (Automatic Sliding Door) Lock and rotate key in applicable direction. Simultaneously, manually push & slide ASD by grabbing top side of ASD. Open both leafs of ASD .
Step 8 OPEN SALOON DOOR: Manually open the Saloon Door Leafs of the concerned Saloon Door .
Step 9 RESTORE SALOON DOOR: After use, Manually close both Saloon Doors leafs. Insert square key anticlockwise to reset position of the EAD .
Step 10 RESTORE ASD: Slide ASD and completely close both ASD leafs from platform side .

II. Operating Cab/Saloon EED at Platform

Egress procedure executed from inside the train towards the platform .

ASD View from Trackside
ERM Handle Rotation
Step 1 UNLOCK EED: Operate the EED from inside the cab/saloon and rotate clockwise to UNLOCK position .
Step 2 OPEN SALOON DOOR: Manually open the Door Leafs of the concerned Saloon Door .
Step 3 OPEN ASD: Push ERM (emergency release mechanism) handles provided on the corresponding ASD and manually slide it's both leafs to open ASD .
Step 4 RESTORE ASD: After use, Slide and completely close both ASD leafs from track side .
Step 5 RESTORE SALOON DOOR: Manually close both Saloon Doors leafs. EED will reset by square key .

NGN Instruction - 22

Rescue Operation of Trains

Push-out & Pull-out Procedures for NRS-1 Trains

This module details the comprehensive protocols for rescuing a defective NRS-1 train using a healthy assisting train . It covers mechanical coupling, pneumatic verification, brake testing in standstill and motion, and final uncoupling procedures across various states of train degradation .

Rescue Directions & Suitability Cases

OCC determines the rescue direction and procedure based on the defective train's power and pneumatic status .

Direction of Rescue Operation

1. Push-out Operation

The assisting Train is coupled to the defective train from the rear cab and pushes it towards the existing direction of travel .

2. Pull-out Operation

The assisting train is coupled to the defective train from the front. After coupling, the train operator changes the cab, and the assisting train pulls the defective train in the existing direction of travel .

Defective Train Status Categories

Case-I POWER & AIR AVAILABLE: No traction possible. Battery Voltage is available (Train is in Wake Up condition), VCB is close, and MR & SR Pressure is available .
Case-II POWER AVAILABLE, NO AIR: No traction possible. Battery Voltage is available (Train is in Wake Up condition), but VCB is open and MR & SR Pressure is not available .
Case-III DEAD TRAIN (SLEEP): No traction possible. Battery Voltage is not available (Train is in Sleep condition), and MR & SR pressure is not available .

Phase 1: Preparation & Mechanical Coupling

Standard physical coupling procedure applied across all rescue scenarios.

Coupler Overview & Status Indicator
Step 1 APPROACH: Assisting train stops about 10 meters before the defective train. Defective train TO puts Mode Selector at OFF and takes out MASCON key.
Step 2 & 3 COUPLE: After passengers hold handrails and permission is granted, the assisting train couples at a maximum speed of 3 kmph. Max 3 attempts allowed.
Step 4 INSPECTION: Physically check coupling status:
  • 'Train coupled' message on TCMS VDU.
  • Buffing face: No gap.
  • No leakage of Pneumatic Pressure.
  • Check status through coupler status indicator (Observation direction: coupled-aligned, uncoupled-not aligned).
Step 5 PNEUMATIC VERIFICATION: Verify MR and SR continuity by isolating and then opening the MRP cock of the defective train. MR/SR pressure should vent continuously towards the assisting train.

Phase 2: System Initialization & Standstill Testing

Verification of brake loops across the newly coupled consist before motion .

Step 6 CAB SETUP: Defective train TO proceeds to leading cab of rescue direction and sets :
  • Mode Selector to OFF mode .
  • TBC to B-max Position .
  • DEADMAN BYPASS switch to bypass position .
  • SCS and RESET ATC switch to isolate position .
Step 7 STANDSTILL BRAKE TEST: Coordinated test between both TOs :
  • Assisting TO applies EB from TBC and Push button. Defective TO monitors drop/rise in Brake Cylinder Pressure (BCP) and EB lamp .
  • Assisting TO applies and releases Parking Brake. Both monitor on VDU .
  • Defective TO applies EB via push button. (Note: EB can only be reset through Assisting train) .
Step 8 AUTHORIZATION: TO at leading end verifies line of sight is clear. Assisting Train TO obtains authorization from OCC for degraded mode movement .

Phase 3: Motion Testing & Train Movement

Critical steps for moving the combined heavy consist safely .

Step 9 COASTING CHECK: Assisting TO moves TBC to 'Powering minimum' and immediately to 'Coasting'. Check with Defective TO that holding brakes are released (BCP is zero) .
Step 10 MOTION BRAKE TEST (Max 3 kmph):
  • Apply service brake through Assisting TBC .
  • Apply EB through Assisting push button/TBC .
  • Apply EB through Defective train push button . Ensure application/release across both trains in all steps .
Step 11 & 12 MOVEMENT: Move the consist at speed up to 10 Kmph . Braking must be done by TO of assisting train only through TBC . Maintain constant radio coordination .
🛑 Critical Braking Warnings

The brakes must be controlled from the TBC of the assisting Train only to ensure application in the whole consist .

The TO of the defective train shall remain alert at the front end with hand on the EB push button to guide the assisting train. If applied by TBC from Assisting Train without coordination, there is a risk of parting the coupled train .

Phase 4: Detrainment & Uncoupling

Final procedures for securing passengers and separating the consist .

Step 13 & 14 DETRAINMENT & STABLING: Stop the train at the next station platform such that all doors of the defective train are at the platform . Defective TO opens doors to detrain passengers, makes announcements, and closes doors . Assisting TO drives consist to nearest depot/siding as per OCC instructions .
Step 15 UNCOUPLING SEQUENCE:
  • Ensure coupling end cab is activated. Normalize DSD switch, put TBC in braking position, and MS to OFF .
  • Disconnect the cab-to-cab jumper cable .
  • Assisting TO gives uncouple command from push button .
  • Drive train in reverse for a small distance after checking track clearance, then inform OCC .

Critical Variations for Dead Train Rescue

Specific procedural changes when rescuing a train with No Air (Case II) or No Battery/Sleep (Case III) .

Case-II Variations (No MR/SR Air)

Because pneumatic pressure is depleted, the connection timing of the rescue jumper cable changes .

  • After successful mechanical coupling, make both Trains sleep .
  • Defective Train TO leaves via cab door, goes to the track, and connects the cab-to-cab Rescue Jumper cable .
  • After connecting the cable, wake up both trains and verify MOP communication before proceeding to brake testing .

Case-III Variations (Dead Battery / Sleep)

The defective train has absolutely no power. The assisting train must supply it .

  • Make Assisting Train Sleep after mechanical coupling. Connect jumper cable, then Wake Up Assisting Train .
  • No Comm: Cab-to-cab MOP communication functionality will not be available .
  • Loss of EB Control: Emergency Brake will be controlled by Assisting Train only. No EB application or resetting facility will be available from the Defective Train .

NGN Instruction - 23

Operation & Restoration of Detrainment Door

Emergency Evacuation Procedures for NRS-1 Trains

In NMRC Aqua trains, the emergency evacuation door system is mounted on the right side of the driver's cab (viewed from inside the train) . The system consists of an evacuation door and an evacuation ramp, allowing passengers to safely evacuate to the track in emergencies .

A. Opening Procedure (Access & Deployment)

Initial steps to unlock the covers and deploy the main structural door .

01. STEP 1
Remove Dummy Cover

Unlock the four quick rotatory locks manually, then remove the ramp dummy cover and keep it behind the cab/saloon partition wall in the saloon area (keep it aside at a secure and safe place) .
02. STEP 2
Unlock the Ramp Cover

Open the transparent cover plate to rotate the red handle clockwise to the open position to unlock the lock .
03. STEP 3
Open the Ramp Cover

Lift up the ramp cover using the handrail on the cover and release the locking system .
04. STEP 4
Open Cab Detrainment Door

Push the door to a certain angle (about 25°) . At this time the air spring will support and push the door until it is fully opened (Final angle about 65°) .

A. Opening Procedure (Ramp Deployment)

Deploying the passenger ramp and securing the legs onto the track .

05. STEP 5
Unlock the Ramp

Pull up the red handle in the ramp to unlock the ramp .
06. STEP 6
Unfold the Ramp

Push the ramp front away to a certain distance (about 200mm) . At this time the air spring will support and push the ramp until it is fully opened .
07. STEP 7
Secure the Legs

Lay down the 5th level step and confirm that the supporting legs and expansion legs are opened .
Passenger Evacuation Duty

The Train Operator shall guide the passengers for safe detrainment through the ramp .

The Train Operator shall make sure that all passengers have come out of the train .

B. Restoring Procedure (Retracting Ramp)

Mechanical steps for folding the ramp structure back into the train using specialized tools .

01. STEP 1
Fold the Steps

Retract the overlapping-on-track device in sequence under the car . First fold the 5th level step .
02. STEP 2
Retract the Legs

Then press down the top of the detent with one hand and lift the supporting leg with another hand, and retract both supporting legs present on both left and right .
03. STEP 3/4
Retract the Ramp (Red Dot Key)

Take out the ratchet spanner and retraction keys from behind the dummy cover . Insert the ramp retraction key (with red dot) inside the groove on the left pillar, fit the spanner, and lock the switch . Ratchet spanner has to be pushed downwards and pull up (spanner is free when pulling up) until the whole ramp has retracted to its original position .
05. STEP 5
Lock the Ramp

Then push the red handle down to lock the ramp .

NGN Instruction - 26

Functionality of Smoke Detection

Internal & External Smoke Protocols in NRS-1 Trains

Internal and External smoke detectors are provided in NRS-1 trains . Automatic operation of dampers is performed in case of internal/external smoke detection, however train operator can also manually operate fresh air dampers in case of external smoke only .

FDU Detector Screen Overview

Reference interface for smoke detection and HVAC damper controls .

FDU Detector Screen

The FDU Detector screen displays real-time smoke detection status across all car units (CR1001, etc.), HVAC smoke status, fresh air damper (FAD) and return air damper (RAD) positions .

Available Soft Keys: RESET, BYPASS, HVACFAD CLOSE .

i. On Outside Smoke Detection

Standard protocols when external smoke or fire is detected .

Step 1 DETECTION & ALARM: Smoke on the outside is detected by the smoke detector . An outside smoke signal is sent to TCMS through HVAC . A pop-up and smoke alarm will be indicated on TCMS display with information of car no. & detector no .
Step 2 ACKNOWLEDGMENT: This alarm shall be acknowledged by Train operator (TO), and same shall be informed to OCC by TO .
Step 3 AUTOMATIC DAMPERS: TCMS sends signal to all HVACs for automatically closing the fresh air dampers (FAD) in order to prevent smoke and heat being drawn into the train .
Step 4 TRAIN MOVEMENT: TO shall further follow the OCC instructions . If TO does not receive any instruction from OCC to act, he shall move away train from fire area .
Step 5 RESTORATION: When outside smoke is clear (it shall be checked/confirmed by TO), TO shall operate RESET soft button available on TCMS-FDU page for opening all HVACs FAD, keeping OCC duly informed .

ii. On Inside Saloon & Cab Area Smoke Detection

Emergency procedures for internal fires or smoke within passenger/cab areas .

Step 1 DETECTION & ALARM: If inside/cab area smoke is detected, an inside smoke signal is sent to TCMS through HVAC . A pop-up and smoke alarm will be indicated on TCMS display with car no. & detector no . Acknowledge and inform OCC .
Step 2 AUTOMATIC DAMPERS: TCMS sends signal to all HVACs for automatically closing the return air dampers (RAD) & fully opening the fresh air dampers (FAD) in order to prevent re-circulation of smoke and heat present in train .
Step 3 STATION EVACUATION TARGET: TO shall follow OCC instructions . If no instructions received, all efforts shall be made by TO to reach the next station to the extent possible . At next station all required action/help to be arranged by OCC .
Step 4 EMERGENCY FIREFIGHTING: In case TO is unable to reach next station, he shall plan/arrange for emergency evacuation of passengers in coordination with OCC . Thereafter use fire extinguishers available in the train (1 in each cab, 4 in each car) to extinguish fire to the extent possible till further assistance arrives .

iii. Before Automatic Detection (Manual FAD Close)

Manual damper control when smoke is identified visually or reported by OCC prior to automatic detection .

Step 1 FORCE CLOSE: When TO identifies outside smoke or when informed by OCC, TO is to close fresh air dampers forcefully through the 'FAD CLOSE' soft button available on the TCMS-FDU page, duly informing OCC .
Step 2 RESET DAMPERS: When outside smoke is clear (checked/confirmed by TO), TO shall operate RESET soft button available on the TCMS-FDU page for opening of all HVACs FAD after informing OCC .

iv. Bypass of Detectors (False Alarms)

Protocols for resetting or bypassing malfunctioning detectors .

Step 1 ASSESS ALARM: When smoke is detected, TO shall inform OCC and judge whether real fire happened or is it false alarm, informing observation to OCC .
Step 2 FIRST RESET: If identified as a dummy alarm (malfunction operation of detector), TO shall reset the detectors by pressing RESET soft button available on the TCMS-FDU page, duly informing OCC .
Step 3 SECOND MALFUNCTION (BYPASS): Upon second time malfunction of detector, TO can bypass the particular detector by pressing the BYPASS soft button available on the TCMS-FDU page, keeping OCC informed . Bypassed detector shall not detect smoke further till normalized .
Step 4 DEPOT NORMALIZATION: Normalization of bypassed detector shall be done by depot maintenance team by RESET button after maintenance . At next station, OCC shall arrange thorough examination of train by maintenance staff .
⚠️ Operational Safeguard Note

Failure of backup support (smoke detection/damper control) and/or TO's decision to bypass sensors through VDU-HMI should not stop the Train operator from doing routine duties to safeguard passengers by following the NMRC Disaster Management Manual .

NGN Instruction - 27

Emergency Operation when TCMS Fails

Actions when TCMS Stops Functioning and Train is Unable to Move

This module outlines the mandatory emergency protocols for Train Operators when the Train Control and Management System (TCMS) completely stops functioning, resulting in a total loss of movement capability on the NRS-1 train .

Emergency Procedure for TCMS Total Failure

Mandatory sequential steps to be executed by the Train Operator upon encountering a critical TCMS failure .

Step 1 CONTACT OCC: Once T.O. finds any failures in TCMS system component/equipment/circuit element/software/communication systems etc., resulting in loss of intended function, by any fault, T.O. shall contact OCC to get instruction for next step of action .
Step 2 PASSENGER ANNOUNCEMENT: T.O. shall make announcement to passengers, informing about train failure and requesting them to stay calm .
Step 3 RESET & EVACUATION: On instruction from OCC, T.O. may try 'Battery reset' / 'Aux. OFF/ON' duly informing the passengers to reset TCMS . If, train is still unable to move, T.O. shall inform OCC and shall prepare for emergency evacuation in coordination with OCC .
